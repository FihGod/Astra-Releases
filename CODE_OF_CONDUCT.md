# Community code of conduct

Treat other people with respect. Astra's GitHub discussions, issues, pull requests, and support spaces should be welcoming and useful.

Be constructive, stay on topic, and respect differing experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam, impersonation, and sharing someone's private information are not acceptable. Never post passwords, license keys, payment details, or authentication codes.

Report concerns privately to Astra staff through a Discord support ticket. If a staff member is involved, contact the server owner privately. Include only the information needed to investigate, and do not post accusations or identifying details publicly.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban accounts based on the severity and repetition of misconduct. Reports will be handled with care, and personal information will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond.
